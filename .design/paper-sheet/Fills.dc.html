the one decision left
Do the role fills survive the pencil?
Once the ground is one value, a filled box is the only thing left that is a surface. Keeping the fills is safer; dropping them is what actually looks like pencil. The good news is that role identity survives either way — the border tokens are already five distinct hues at nearly one lightness.
Keep the fills
safer, still reads as boxes
A filled box on a flat ground is still a small panel, so the "one surface" idea is only half true. But the roles land instantly, and check:flowchart-palette keeps measuring exactly what it measures today.
Drop them
true line art — my pick
Nothing on the page is a surface — it is all line on one sheet. The role hues move to the stroke and get +0.2 of weight to carry it. External keeps its dashed edge, which is doing more work now than it was.
